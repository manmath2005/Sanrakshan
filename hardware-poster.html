HOW SANRAKSHAN WORKS: SYSTEM OPERATION & SAFETY FLOW

Interactive demonstration of the pre-ride safety checks, alcohol interlock, wireless ESP-NOW, and crash SOS

Sanrakshan Project Working Demonstration Recording 1080p HD
1

Helmet Wear Detection (IR Sensor)

Rider puts on the helmet. The TCRT5000 IR sensor detects head proximity and outputs LOW on GPIO 13. Helmet buzzer gives a single confirmation chirp.

2

Sobriety Breathalyzer Check (MQ-3)

MQ-3 measures breath alcohol concentration. If raw reading is below 800 ADC (< 0.03% BAC), the interlock controller issues an AUTHORIZED status.

3

Ignition Key Switch ON $\rightarrow$ DC Motor Spins

When the ignition key is turned ON (GPIO 4 connected to GND), the ESP32 activates the 5V relay. Power flows to the DC motor and the bike engine starts.

4

Safety Interlock Lockout (Violation)

If the rider removes the helmet or consumes alcohol (≥ 800 ADC), the relay cuts power in < 10ms. The DC motor stops, buzzer sounds, and blue LED blinks rapidly.

5

Crash Impact Detection & Autonomous Rescue

MPU-6050 detects impact (≥ 4.0G). The motor cuts off instantly. A 3-second abort window buzzer sounds; if not cancelled, an emergency SMS with live GPS coordinates is dispatched to family.
