Subsystem A: Wearable Helmet Transmitter Unit
NODE 1: TRANSMITTER

ESP32 DevKit V1 (30-Pin)

ESP-WROOM-32 | Xtensa Dual-Core
Samples all helmet safety sensors at 100Hz and transmits instantaneous zero-latency packets over ESP-NOW (<18ms).
VOLTAGE (V) 5.0V VIN / 3.3V Logic
CURRENT (A) 80mA typ / 350mA peak
CHIN GUARD

MQ-3 Alcohol Sensor

Tin Dioxide (SnO2) Breathalyzer
Heats SnO2 ceramic coil to 200°C; detects breath alcohol molecules. Immobilizes starter if ADC ≥ 800.
VOLTAGE (V) 5.0V DC ±0.1V
CURRENT (A) 150mA (Heater Active)
TOP CROWN

GY-521 MPU-6050 (6-DoF)

3-Axis Accel + 3-Axis Gyro (I2C)
Calculates vector magnitude G = √(ax²+ay²+az²). Triggers zero-delay SOS if impact ≥ 4.0G.
VOLTAGE (V) 3.3V – 5.0V DC
CURRENT (A) 3.8mA (Ultra Low-Power)
FOREHEAD LEVEL

TCRT5000 Optical IR Sensor

950nm Infrared Diode + LM393
Non-contact skin/head proximity reflection check. Outputs clean LOW (0V) only when helmet is securely on head.
VOLTAGE (V) 3.3V – 5.0V DC
CURRENT (A) 15mA – 20mA
LATERAL FLANK

18650 Li-ion + TP4056 Type-C

3.7V 2600mAh + DW01A Protection
Lightweight wearable power source. Safe 1A CC/CV USB-C charging with over-discharge cut-off at < 2.5V.
VOLTAGE (V) 3.7V Nom (4.2V Max)
CAPACITY / AMPS 2600mAh / 1.0A Charge
HELMET POWER RAIL

MT3608 DC-DC Step-Up Booster

1.2MHz High-Efficiency Boost
Steps up 3.7V battery voltage to a regulated 5.0V rail to power the MQ-3 alcohol heater and ESP32 VIN.
VOLTAGE (V) 3.7V In → 5.0V Regulated
MAX OUTPUT (A) 2.0A Maximum
Subsystem B: Motorcycle Receiver & Actuator Interlock
NODE 2: RECEIVER & BLE

ESP32 DevKit V1 (Bike Unit)

ESP-NOW RX + BLE 4.2 GATT Server
Receives helmet telemetry over ESP-NOW, drives the 0.96" OLED HUD and 5V Relay, and streams BLE data to phone.
VOLTAGE (V) 5.0V VIN / 3.3V Logic
CURRENT (A) 120mA typ / 350mA peak
CONSOLE HUD

0.96" I2C OLED Display

SSD1306 | 128x64 Pixels (4-Pin)
Displays real-time helmet connection status, breath alcohol ADC, impact G-Force, and ignition status to the rider.
VOLTAGE (V) 3.3V – 5.0V DC
CURRENT (A) 20mA (All Pixels Lit)
STARTER INTERLOCK

5V Optocoupler Relay Module

SRD-05VDC-SL-C (Active HIGH)
Galvanically isolates the 3.3V ESP32 from the 12V starter motor. Disables ignition if helmet is off, rider is drunk, or after a crash.
COIL VOLTAGE (V) 5.0V DC
COIL / CONTACT (A) 70mA Coil / 10A 250VAC
BIKE POWER STEP-DOWN

LM2596 DC-DC Buck Converter

Step-Down Power Regulator Module
Steps down motorcycle 12V battery input to regulated 5.0V 2A to power the ESP32 receiver, OLED, and relay coil.
VOLTAGE (V) 12V In → 5.0V Output
OUTPUT CURRENT (A) 2.0A Continuous (3A Peak)
ENGINE SIMULATOR

DC Geared Motor & Wheel

3V–6V BO Motor (or 12V 300 RPM)
Simulates the vehicle starter/engine. Rotates when relay closes; stops immediately when ignition locks or on crash.
VOLTAGE (V) 3.0V – 6.0V (or 12V DC)
OPERATING AMPS (A) 150mA – 350mA Loaded
AUDIO ALARM

5V Active Piezo Buzzer

KY-012 Continuous Tone Alarm
Audible alarm beeper. Emits continuous alarm when alcohol is detected or during 3-second post-crash abort window.
VOLTAGE (V) 3.3V – 5.0V DC
CURRENT (A) 25mA – 30mA Active
TOTAL SYSTEM POWER: Helmet Unit: ~1.25W (3.7V Li-ion boosted to 5V) | Bike Unit: ~2.1W (12V vehicle battery)
WIRELESS PROTOCOLS: ESP-NOW Broadcast (<18ms latency) • BLE 4.2 GATT Telemetry Server
